Featured app

Imam Direct

Talk directly to verified Imams, book online Quran classes, and keep your daily Deen with prayer times, Azan reminders, Roza, Qibla and Tasbih, all in one simple app. Available in English, Hindi, Urdu and Arabic.

Privacy Policy
🕌

Prayer times & Azan

Automatic times for your city with 12 calculation methods and Hanafi or Shafi Asr. Azan, advance and Jamaat reminders even when the app is closed. Jumu'ah replaces Zohar on Fridays.

⏱️

Your masjid's time

Set your own masjid time for any prayer, including how much it shifts each day. Turn Azan or Jamaat on or off per prayer.

🌙

Roza, Tahajjud & Jumu'ah

Sehri and Iftar reminders, automatic or by your masjid time, plus separate Tahajjud and Jumu'ah reminders.

📅

Islamic calendar & Qibla

Hijri calendar with important dates like Ramadan, Eid and Ashura, moon-sighting adjustment and a compass Qibla finder.

📿

Tasbih & Wazifa

Tasbih counter with vibration, rounds and your own daily wazifa. Your record stays only on your phone.

📖

Islamic Learning

Stories, audio, videos and books for children and families, in English, Hinglish or Urdu.

👳

Talk to Imams

Find approved Imams by city, language and fiqh, see their ratings and reviews, listen to their recitation, then book a consultation or Quran tuition. See Imam Direct

🎓

Online Quran classes

Pick days, time slot and start date. Once the Imam confirms, classes are scheduled automatically with a Google Meet link for every session.

📱

Widgets & languages

Home-screen widgets for the next Azan and tomorrow's Sehri & Iftar. Use the app in English, Hindi, Urdu or Arabic, for Sunni or Shia fiqh.

Free to use. Family-friendly ads keep the app free; subscribe monthly or yearly to remove them. An account is needed only to consult or book an Imam.

New in Imam Direct

Talk directly to verified Imams

Learn Quran and ask your questions from trusted Imams, online. Every Imam is checked by our team before they appear in the app, and every review is checked before it is published.

✅

Checked Imams

Imams apply with their qualification, a recitation and their times. Our team checks each one; experienced Imams earn the Verified badge.

⭐

Honest ratings

Only students who attended a class can rate it. Reviews show the first name only and go live after our team checks them. The best-rated Imams appear first.

🗓️

Book in a few taps

See fees and free times upfront, pick your days and time, and get a Google Meet link for every class, with reminders before it starts.

🧒

Safe for children

Classes for students under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent. Report any concern from the app; child-safety reports are handled first.

🔁

Reschedule or cancel

Move a class to another free time, or cancel before the first class for a full refund. Refund policy

For Imams
