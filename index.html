Featured app

Azan & Deen

Prayer times, Azan reminders, Roza, Qibla, Tasbih, Islamic stories and online Quran classes with trusted Imams, all in one simple app. Available in English, Hindi, Urdu and Arabic.

Privacy Policy
🕌

Prayer times & Azan

Automatic times for your city with 12 calculation methods and Hanafi or Shafi Asr. Azan, advance and Jamaat reminders even when the app is closed. Jumu'ah replaces Zohar on Fridays.

⏱️

Your masjid's time

Set your own masjid time for any prayer, including how much it shifts each day. Turn Azan or Jamaat on or off per prayer.

🌙

Roza, Tahajjud & Jumu'ah

Sehri and Iftar reminders, automatic or by your masjid time, plus separate Tahajjud and Jumu'ah reminders.

📅

Islamic calendar & Qibla

Hijri calendar with important dates like Ramadan, Eid and Ashura, moon-sighting adjustment and a compass Qibla finder.

📿

Tasbih & Wazifa

Tasbih counter with vibration, rounds and your own daily wazifa. Your record stays only on your phone.

📖

Islamic Learning

Stories, audio, videos and books for children and families, in English, Hinglish or Urdu.

👳

Imam Connect

Find approved Imams by city, language and fiqh. Listen to their recitation, then request a consultation or Quran tuition.

🎓

Online Quran classes

Pick days, time slot and start date. Once the Imam confirms, classes are scheduled automatically with a Google Meet link for every session.

📱

Widgets & languages

Home-screen widgets for the next Azan and tomorrow's Sehri & Iftar. Use the app in English, Hindi, Urdu or Arabic, for Sunni or Shia fiqh.

Free to use. Family-friendly ads keep the app free; subscribe monthly or yearly to remove them. An account is needed only for Imam Connect.
