Expense Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixelminar Technologies ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Expense Tracker Android app (the "app"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

Expense Tracker is in development. This policy describes how the app is designed to work and will be updated before release if anything changes. Your financial records are stored only on your device; the app does not connect to your bank and does not read your SMS messages.

Information the app uses

DataWhy it is usedWhere it is stored
Expenses, income, categories and notes you enterTo track spending and show reportsOn your device only
Budgets and app settings (currency, theme)To remember your preferencesOn your device only
Exported reports / backups (optional)If you choose to export or back upWherever you choose to save or share them

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in, and we do not collect your email address or phone number.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) – for daily logging reminders and budget alerts.

You can revoke any permission at any time from your device's Settings › Apps › Expense Tracker › Permissions. Some features may stop working without them.

Financial disclaimer

Expense Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not provide financial, tax or investment advice.

No ads, no tracking

The app does not show advertisements, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data. To request deletion of any data held by us or our service providers, see our Data Deletion page.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
