Azan Reminder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixelminar Technologies ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Azan Reminder Android app (the "app"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

Azan Reminder calculates prayer times on your device. Your prayer settings and reminders are stored only on your phone. The app has no user accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Information the app uses

DataWhy it is usedWhere it is stored
City you enter, or your approximate location if you turn on Use device locationTo calculate prayer, Sehri and Iftar times for your areaOn your device only. Location is never sent to us
Prayer settings: calculation method, Asr method, manual times and time correctionsTo show prayer times the way you wantOn your device only
Reminder settings: Azan alerts, Azan sound choice, Jamaat, Tahajjud, Jumu'ah, Sehri and Iftar remindersTo deliver the reminders you turn onOn your device only
Imam Connect interest (if you tap I'm interested)To remember your choice for when the feature launchesOn your device only
Network request details (such as IP address) when loading Islamic Learning contentTo download published stories, audio and worksheetsOur content provider, Supabase, as part of normal server logs

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in, and we do not collect your email address or phone number.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to play the Azan or a short sound and show prayer, Jamaat, Sehri and Iftar reminders.
  • Location (optional) – only if you turn on Use device location, to calculate times for where you are. You can type your city instead.
  • Internet – to load Islamic Learning content. Prayer times and reminders work offline.

You can revoke any permission at any time from your device's Settings › Apps › Azan Reminder › Permissions. Some features may stop working without them.

Islamic Learning content

Stories, audio and worksheets in the Islamic Learning section are published by us and loaded from our content provider, Supabase. The app only downloads content; it does not upload any of your personal data. Content is reviewed before it is published.

Accuracy of prayer times

Prayer times are calculated using standard astronomical methods and may differ by a few minutes from your local mosque. You can adjust each prayer time manually in the app.

No ads, no tracking

The app does not show advertisements, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data. To request deletion of any data held by us or our service providers, see our Data Deletion page.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
