Med Reminder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixelminar Technologies ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Med Reminder Android app (the "app"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

Medicine and health information you enter is personal and sensitive. It is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to our servers, sold, or used for advertising.

Information the app uses

DataWhy it is usedWhere it is stored
Medicine names, doses and schedulesTo show reminders at the right timeOn your device only
Dose history (taken / skipped)To show your history and adherenceOn your device only
Optional profile names (e.g. family members)To manage reminders for more than one personOn your device only
Backup file (optional)If you choose to export/back up your dataWherever you choose to save it (e.g. your Google Drive)
Crash and diagnostic data (anonymous)To find and fix bugs; never includes your medicine dataOur crash-reporting provider, if enabled

The app does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to remind you to take your medicine.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) – to deliver reminders at the exact time you set.
  • Run at startup – to re-schedule reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Camera (optional) – only if you choose to take a photo of a medicine. Photos stay on your device.

You can revoke any permission at any time from your device's Settings › Apps › Med Reminder › Permissions. Some features may stop working without them.

Medical disclaimer

Med Reminder is a reminder tool only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow the advice of your doctor or pharmacist, and do not rely on the app as your only way of remembering critical medication.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Play services to be distributed and updated. Where noted above, the app may also use the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics (anonymous crash reports and usage statistics)
  • Google AdMob (if the app shows ads)

These services may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address and diagnostic data. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Google › Ads.

Data security

Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Any data sent to third-party services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data. To request deletion of any data held by us or our service providers, see our Data Deletion page.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
