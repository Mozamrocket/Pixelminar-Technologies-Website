Data Deletion

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This page explains how you can delete your data from apps published by Pixelminar Technologies: Azan & Deen, Dawai Dost and Expense Tracker.

Azan & Deen: Imam Connect account

If you signed in with Google to use Imam Connect or online classes, you can delete your account and its data at any time:

  1. Open Azan & Deen and go to the Imam tab.
  2. Tap the account icon.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Or email support@pixelminar.com from the Google account you used. We delete your account, name and email, Imam profile (including photo, recitation, mobile number and address), requests and class schedules within 30 days. Reports you made may be kept in anonymised form if needed to keep the service safe.

Data stored on your device

Apart from the Imam Connect account above, our apps do not require an account, and their data is stored only on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  1. Using the Delete / Reset data option inside the app settings, or
  2. Going to Settings › Apps › [App name] › Storage › Clear data, or
  3. Uninstalling the app.

All of these permanently delete the app's local data. We cannot recover it afterwards.

Requesting deletion of other data

If you want us to delete any data held by us (for example, emails you have sent us), email support@pixelminar.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include:

  • The app name
  • The email address you contacted us from (if any)

We will process your request within 30 days.

Dawai Dost family alerts

If you linked family members in Dawai Dost, messages passed through ntfy.sh are deleted automatically after about 12 hours. To stop sharing, remove the linked family member in the app, or ask them to remove you, and clear the app's data on the phones involved.
