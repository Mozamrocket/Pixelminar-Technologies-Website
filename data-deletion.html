Data Deletion

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This page explains how you can delete your data from apps published by Pixelminar Technologies: Azan & Prayer Times, Med Reminder and Expense Tracker.

Data stored on your device

Our apps do not require an account, and almost all data is stored only on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  1. Using the Delete / Reset data option inside the app settings, or
  2. Going to Settings › Apps › [App name] › Storage › Clear data, or
  3. Uninstalling the app.

All of these permanently delete the app's local data. We cannot recover it afterwards.

Requesting deletion of other data

If you want us to delete any data held by us or our service providers (for example, crash reports, or emails you have sent us), email support@pixelminar.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include:

  • The app name
  • The email address you contacted us from (if any)

We will process your request within 30 days. Anonymous crash and analytics data cannot be linked to you and is automatically deleted by our providers after their standard retention period (typically 90 days or less).
