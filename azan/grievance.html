Azan & Deen – Grievances

Last updated: 16 November 2026

As required by the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, here is how to raise a complaint about the Azan & Deen app, an Imam, a booking, a payment or your personal data.

Grievance Officer

Muzammil Nasir, Pixelminar Technologies
Email: support@pixelminar.com
Phone: +91 73791 92447
Address: 544/438, K.L Kydganj, Kydganj, Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh – 211003, India

How to complain

  • In the app: Imam Connect › account icon › Help & complaints, or use Report on an Imam's profile or a booking.
  • By email to the address above. Please include your sign-in email, the Imam's name and the booking date, if any.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and give you a reference.
  • We resolve it within 30 days (sooner when we can), and tell you what we did.
  • Anything that may put a child at risk is acted on immediately, and reported to the authorities where the law requires.
  • Requests about your personal data (to see, correct or delete it, or withdraw consent) are handled the same way. You can also delete your account in the app.
