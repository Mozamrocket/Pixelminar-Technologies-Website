1. The app

The app gives prayer times, Azan reminders, a Qibla direction, a Tasbih counter, an Islamic calendar and Islamic learning content. Prayer times are calculated and may differ slightly from your local mosque; please confirm with your mosque when in doubt.

2. Imam Direct is a marketplace

  • Imam Direct lets you book a consultation or Quran tuition with an Imam over Google Meet. Imams are independent teachers, not our employees or agents. We are an online platform (marketplace) that connects you; the consultation or class is a service given by the Imam.
  • Each Imam's name, city, languages, experience, qualification, fees and free times are shown on their profile, as the Imam gave them. We review applications before listing an Imam and may mark an Imam "ID checked" or "Verified". Each Imam has a level based on on-time classes, time on Imam Direct and reviews, and the highest consultation fee an Imam may ask depends on that level (at least ₹300). We do not guarantee the content, quality or outcome of any consultation or class.
  • Religious opinions shared in a consultation are the Imam's own. For important matters please also consult your local scholar.

3. Booking and paying

  • The price you see at checkout is the full price: the Imam's fee for the days and months you chose. We add no hidden charges.
  • You may pay the Imam directly after the first class (outside the app), or pay online in the app. Online payments are processed by Razorpay; the Imam receives their share (87%) after the first class and we keep 13% as our platform fee.
  • Cancellations and refunds follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

4. Ratings and reviews

  • After a class has taken place you can rate the Imam (1–5 stars) and write a review. Our team checks reviews before they are shown; we may refuse or remove any review that is false, abusive, off-topic or shares personal information.
  • Approved reviews show the name you give with them, the rating and your words to other users (never your email), and the Imam may reply. Reviews and ratings also count towards the Imam's level.
  • Write only honest reviews of classes you actually had. Do not post reviews in return for money, discounts or favours, or to harm an Imam unfairly.
