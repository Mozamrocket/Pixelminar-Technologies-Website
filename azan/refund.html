Imam Direct – Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers consultations and Quran tuition booked in the Imam Direct app (Imam Direct). It is part of our Terms of Use.

1. Cancelling a booking

  • You can cancel a booking any time before the first class: open Imam Direct › My Consultations and classes and tap Cancel booking.
  • If you paid online, you get a full refund of the amount paid. There is no cancellation charge.
  • Once the first class has started, the booking can no longer be cancelled in the app. For tuition of more than one month, contact us before the next month starts and we will review a refund of the unused months fairly.
  • You can also reschedule a class to another free time of the same Imam from the app.

2. When the Imam cannot teach

  • If the time you booked was taken by someone else at the same moment, the booking is not made and an online payment is refunded in full automatically.
  • If the Imam misses a class or cancels, report it in the app or email us. We will arrange another time with the Imam or refund you in full for the missed class(es).

3. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the same UPI account or card you paid with, through Razorpay. They usually reach you within 5–7 working days (your bank may take a little longer). If you paid the Imam directly, any refund is between you and the Imam; we will help if there is a dispute.

4. Subscriptions

The ad-free subscription is bought and refunded through Google Play or the App Store under their own refund rules.
