Imam Direct – Imam Terms & Code of Conduct

Last updated: 16 November 2026

These terms apply to every Imam, Aalim or teacher ("Imam") who applies to and is listed on Imam Direct in the Imam Direct app, run by Pixelminar Technologies ("we"). They are in addition to our Terms of Use.

1. Your relationship with us

  • You are an independent teacher, not our employee or agent. You set your own fees (within the app's limits) and free times, and you are responsible for your own taxes.
  • For bookings paid online we keep a platform fee of 10%; 90% is paid to your Razorpay linked account after the first class. Bookings paid directly are between you and the student.
  • We may approve, pause or remove your profile at any time, especially after a complaint, missed classes or a breach of these terms.

2. Your details

  • Give true details: name, photo, qualification, experience and languages. We may ask to see a government ID (on a video call) before you can teach children; your profile then shows "ID checked".
  • Your mobile number, address and email are never shown to users.

3. Classes

  • Join every class on time from the app (tap Join). Your points and level depend on it; missed classes lower them and may lead to a refund to the student and removal.
  • Teach with sincerity, correct Islamic knowledge and respect for every student's maslak and family.

4. Child safety (must follow)

  • With a student under 18, keep every class in the app's Google Meet link, with a parent or guardian able to be present. Never ask a child to switch to another app, call or chat privately.
  • Never ask a child for personal contact details, photos, money or gifts, and never share any. Do not record a class without the parent's written permission.
  • Use only respectful words. Any abuse, harassment or inappropriate behaviour with a child leads to immediate removal and will be reported to the police as required by law (including the POCSO Act).
  • If you notice that a child may be at risk, tell us at once.

5. Conduct

No hate speech, insults to other maslaks or faiths, political campaigning, selling unrelated products, or asking students to pay you outside the agreed fee.

6. Data

Use students' details only to teach them, and delete them when you stop teaching them. See our Privacy Policy.
