Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the website and all mobile applications published by Pixelminar Technologies ("we", "us", "our") on Google Play.

Our approach

We build apps that collect as little data as possible. Our apps do not require an account, and the information you enter (such as prayer settings, medicine reminders or expenses) is stored on your own device.

This website

This website does not use cookies, does not have user accounts and does not collect personal information. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security purposes. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
